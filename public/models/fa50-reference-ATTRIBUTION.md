# FA-50 exterior reconstruction

`fa50-reference.glb` is original geometry made for DAPA DASHBOARD from the visible exterior in the public photograph **Light Combat FA-50 Fighting Eagle**, photo by **ROKAF**, published by **Korea Aerospace Industries**. It is an approximate exhibition asset, not a manufacturer CAD model, a scan, or a verified cockpit. Materials, seams, landing gear and seats are reconstructed estimates. No image pixels are embedded in the GLB.

Photo source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Light_Combat_FA-50_Fighting_Eagle.jpg
Original publisher: https://www.flickr.com/photos/koreaaero/12201649244
Photo license, verified on Commons: **CC BY 2.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/
Reconstruction geometry and generator: **CC BY 2.0**, credit DAPA DASHBOARD and the source photographer above; changes: exterior geometry, simplified materials and illustrative internal seats generated from public exterior reference.

Overall nominal length 13.13 m, wingspan 9.44 m, height 4.81 m follow the KAI 2025 official brochure dimension drawings. Individual local measurements are estimated, not engineering data.

Official reference: https://www.koreaaero.com/VIRTUAL_EX/PDF/FA50_ExportVersion/FA-50_ENG_BROCHURE_202510.pdf
Official product page: https://www.koreaaero.com/EN/Business/FA50.aspx

Coordinates: metres, Y up, -Z forward, wheel bottom at Y=0. `eye` metadata is an estimated front-seat camera position, not a verified pilot sightline. Landing gear is statically extended; the model has no operational flight dynamics, stores release or combat simulation.
