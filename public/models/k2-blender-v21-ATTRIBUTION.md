# K2 exterior appearance study · recovery v21

Model: DAPA DASHBOARD, CC BY 3.0, adapted from the existing exhibition draft.
Original public photo: KBS VIEW – theDEEP, CC BY 3.0.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:K2_Black_Panther_20161106.jpg
Manufacturer reference: https://www.hyundai-rotem.co.kr/en/business/defense/details.do?productCt03=defense0101

The four-view image was regenerated with built-in OpenAI image generation from
that photo after automatic workspace pruning removed the unpublished v20 files.
Hidden/rear/top views, paint and details are AI-inferred. These are NOT measured
views, an authenticated photo record, manufacturer CAD or photogrammetry data.

Blender 4.5.3 welds split vertices, finishes selected armor edges with an
illustrative 12mm bevel, and applies projected exterior atlas UVs. Roughness and
micro-normal maps are independently authored visual treatment, not scanned wear.
Original geometry proportions and Hull/Turret/Gun/RunningGear/Markings pivots are
preserved. Atlas lighting is baked into color and projected details may stretch.
No engineering, driving, firing, armor or real performance simulation is provided.

Editable source: assets/blender/k2-reference-v21.blend in the GitHub repository.
Script: scripts/blender/build-ground-reference.py -- k2.
Scenic ground and HDR: ../materials/rocky-terrain/ATTRIBUTION.md.
