# K2 AI surface study — experimental

Source photograph: [K2 Black Panther 20161106](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:K2_Black_Panther_20161106.jpg), KBS VIEW–theDEEP, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The photograph was used as a reference for an AI-generated four-view atlas; unseen surfaces are inferred, not photographed or measured.

The atlas is projected onto the existing approximate K2 exhibition geometry using Blender 4.5.9 LTS. This is not manufacturer CAD, photogrammetry, a validated reconstruction or a finished photoreal model. Wheel and panel alignment and projection distortion remain unresolved. No Threads author's textures or meshes were copied.

Production model unchanged. Experimental URL: `?system=k2&model=photo-study`.

Reproduction: `scripts/blender/photo_study.py` and `scripts/blender/k2-photo-study.json`. Editable packed Blender source and comparison renders: `artifacts/3d/k2-photo-study/`. The GLB contains one embedded image and preserves the original articulated part hierarchy.
